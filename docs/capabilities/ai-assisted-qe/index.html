Uncertainty guardrail

Insufficient evidence must remain insufficient.

Live validation exposed the risk of over-classification when diagnostic evidence is incomplete. The workflow therefore explicitly supports conservative unknown and low-confidence outcomes rather than forcing a confident root cause.

Incomplete evidence → Do not invent root cause → classification: unknown + confidence: low

Structured-output validation guarantees the shape of the result, not its semantic correctness. Critical semantic judgement therefore remains a human engineering responsibility.

Provider-behavior guardrail

Specific contracts require specific evidence.

The generator must not invent status codes, response fields, validation messages or provider semantics that were not supplied through the available engineering context.

Known mechanism + Unknown response contract → Behavioral expectation + missingContext

This keeps generated test design useful without converting model assumptions into false provider contracts.

Human ownership

AI proposes. Engineering decides.

The framework deliberately separates probabilistic assistance from deterministic authority.

Human

Own engineering decisions

Review semantic correctness, architecture, test intent, evidence sufficiency and final approval.
