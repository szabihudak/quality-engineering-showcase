Planned

AI-assisted Quality Engineering.

AI assistance is planned as an engineering workflow enhancement, not as a replacement for architecture ownership or review.

Human intent → Existing architecture → AI assistance → Review → Validated integration

Planned areas include Copilot, Cursor, ChatGPT-assisted workflows, AI-assisted debugging and OpenAI API fundamentals.

Planned

AI-native QA platform evaluation.

Platform evaluation will focus on engineering behavior rather than simply demonstrating vendor features.

Determinism

Can execution be trusted?

Evaluate whether behavior remains reproducible and explainable.

Observability

Can failures be diagnosed?

Assess how much evidence and technical visibility the platform provides.

Maintenance

What changes over time?

Compare self-healing claims with real maintenance ownership and debugging cost.

Control

What remains engineer-owned?

Understand which responsibilities are abstracted and what control is lost or retained.

Planned

QE Strategy & Leadership.

The roadmap expands from implementing automation toward deciding what quality risks matter and how teams should respond to them.

Strategy

Risk-based quality

Test pyramid, shift-left, shift-right and quality strategy.

Metrics

Quality signals

Flakiness, leakage, MTTD, MTTR and meaningful measurement.

Leadership

Engineering enablement

Mentoring, hiring considerations and technical communication.

Stakeholders

Decision communication

Translate technical quality signals into engineering context.

Planned

System Design, Security & Observability.

The final technical phase expands the quality model toward distributed systems and production-oriented engineering signals.

01

System design

Service boundaries, microservices, environment strategy and quality-signal architecture.

02

Security foundations

OWASP concepts, authorization risks, boundary testing and security-aware automation.

03

Observability

Structured logs, monitoring, Grafana, Kibana and production quality feedback.
